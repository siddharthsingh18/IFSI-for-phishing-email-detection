## 3. Domain evidence precision improvements

Implement an experimental Domain Analyzer V3.

### A. Legitimate secondary domains

Handle legitimate organizational secondary domains without assuming that every hyphenated or unfamiliar domain is malicious.

Use explicit, auditable domain relationships.

Do not create broad wildcard trust rules such as allowing every domain containing a brand name.

### B. Internal corporate sender handling

Distinguish:

- A corporate employee using an internal corporate sender domain.
- An external party impersonating a corporate brand.
- A legitimate third-party provider sending a message on behalf of an organization.

A brand name appearing in the subject or body must not automatically create an identity mismatch.

### C. Legitimate SaaS authentication domains

Support legitimate multi-domain service relationships, including:

- Workday and its tenant authentication domains.
- SAP Concur and legitimate corporate integrations.
- Other known services where explicitly supported by the evidence registry.

Every relationship must be documented with its evidence source and scope.

Do not infer trust from name similarity alone.

### D. Evidence semantics

Keep the distinction between:

- Observed domain facts.
- Structural anomalies.
- Verified organizational relationships.
- Unknown or unavailable reputation information.

Never translate unknown evidence into a clean or trusted verdict.

Do not generate invented maliciousness scores.

Do not automatically classify an email as phishing solely because of one domain anomaly.
