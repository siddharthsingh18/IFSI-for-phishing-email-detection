8. **PII & Sensitive-Data Risks**: Low-to-moderate — Honeypot recipient PII is upstream-masked to `phishing@pot`, but bodies/headers may contain spoofed real employee names or third-party reply-to addresses requiring deterministic PII scrubbing.
9. **Known Overlap with Existing IFSI Datasets**: **ZERO** — Never referenced in `data/manifests/`, `source_manifest.json`, `clean_split_v1`, `rag_index_clean`, `phishfuzzer`, or Phases 4–7.
