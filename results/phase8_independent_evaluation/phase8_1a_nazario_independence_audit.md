# Phase 8.1A: Jose Nazario Phishing Corpus — Historical Data Independence Audit

## 1. Executive Summary & Independence Verdict

This document reports a read-only forensic independence audit of the **Jose Nazario Phishing Corpus** (`https://monkey.org/~jose/phishing/`) against all locally available IFSI datasets, training splits, RAG indices, metadata manifests, and evaluation benchmarks.

### Overall Nazario Independence Status: **UNRESOLVED (`phishing3.mbox` 100% EXHAUSTED; RAW & LATER ARCHIVES ABSENT LOCALLY)**

1. **Historical `phishing3.mbox` Is Completely Exhausted (ZERO Independent Messages Remain)**:
   - Repository metadata in [`data/manifests/nazario.json`](file:///d:/Project/IFSI-for-phishing-email-detection/data/manifests/nazario.json) and [`data/metadata/source_manifest.json`](file:///d:/Project/IFSI-for-phishing-email-detection/data/metadata/source_manifest.json) proves that `https://monkey.org/~jose/phishing/phishing3.mbox` (SHA-256 `b29336d2e31c2dff19639415e98ed2aced5b4d63675ea5e29bea1a7d4e452841`) contained `2,279` raw messages, of which `38` were excluded as empty/placeholder bodies and **all `2,241` usable messages (`100.0%`) were consumed** across historical `review_split_seed42` (`1,745` train + `286` val + `210` test = `2,241`) and historical `nazario_enron` (`441` phishing test records).
   - Re-downloading `phishing3.mbox` would result in **100% historical dataset overlap**.
2. **Active Local `"nazario"` Splits Are Synthetic Templates, NOT Raw `.mbox` Messages**:
   - Forensic inspection of the currently checked-in local files (`clean_split_v1`, `enron_augmented`, `review_seed42`, and `results/rag_index_clean/metadata.json`) reveals that **100% of locally stored `"source": "nazario"` records (`502` in `rag_index_clean`) are deterministic synthetic templates generated by [`tools/data/seed_splits.py`](file:///d:/Project/IFSI-for-phishing-email-detection/tools/data/seed_splits.py)** (`10` `PHISH_TEMPLATES` containing `"Reference ID:"` / `"Notice Code: P-"`), **not** authentic Jose Nazario `.mbox` emails.
3. **Cross-Archive Independence of Later `.mbox` Files (`phishing-2015`–`phishing-2025`) Cannot Be Established Yet**:
   - Neither `data/raw/nazario/phishing3.mbox`, `../v1/data/processed/nazario_enron/nazario_enron_test.jsonl`, nor any later annual Nazario `.mbox` archive exists on disk, and Phase 8.1A strictly prohibits downloading datasets. Because phishing campaigns frequently span adjacent `.mbox` archives and because `phishing3.mbox` text is unavailable locally for shingle/campaign comparison, independence of other Nazario `.mbox` files remains **UNRESOLVED**.

## 4. Overlap & Quality Checks NOT Possible Locally

Because original upstream `.mbox` files and legacy V1/V2 raw split files are absent from disk and cannot be downloaded in Phase 8.1A, the following checks **cannot be performed yet**:

1. **Exact / Normalized / Shingle Overlap Against Historical `phishing3.mbox` (`2,279` Messages)**:
   - Without `data/raw/nazario/phishing3.mbox` (`b29336d2...`), we cannot verify zero duplicate messages or shared phish-kit HTML templates between `phishing3.mbox` and newer annual archives (`phishing-2020`–`phishing-2025`).
2. **Exact Overlap Against Historical `nazario_enron` (`441` Phishing Records) & `phishbench_portable_prompts_v1.jsonl` (`18,868` Prompts)**:
   - Both `../v1/data/processed/nazario_enron/nazario_enron_test.jsonl` (`c1121951...`) and `experiments/inputs/phishbench_portable_prompts_v1.jsonl` (`c3df4691...`) are excluded from git per [`PUBLIC_RELEASE.md`](file:///d:/Project/IFSI-for-phishing-email-detection/PUBLIC_RELEASE.md) and absent locally.
3. **Intra-Archive Campaign Burstiness & Non-Phishing Spam Contamination in Modern Nazario `.mbox` Files**:
   - Historical metadata in [`source_manifest.json`](file:///d:/Project/IFSI-for-phishing-email-detection/data/metadata/source_manifest.json) (lines 101–107) recorded `304` train and `341` val near-duplicate pairs within `phishing3.mbox` alone at `0.85` Jaccard. Modern annual Nazario `.mbox` files also occasionally include generic spam, malware-attachment-only stubs, or broken MIME parts that cannot be quantified prior to an authorized pilot inspection.
4. **Source-Label & Honeypot Header Confounding**:
   - As explicitly documented in [`source_manifest.json`](file:///d:/Project/IFSI-for-phishing-email-detection/data/metadata/source_manifest.json) (lines 95–99) and [`config/dataset_gates.yaml`](file:///d:/Project/IFSI-for-phishing-email-detection/config/dataset_gates.yaml), using Nazario exclusively for `phishing` (`100%`) against a single corporate archive for `legitimate` (`100%`) introduces a severe source/header confound (e.g., `Delivered-To:` / `To: jose@monkey.org` vs. `@enron.com` PST headers).
